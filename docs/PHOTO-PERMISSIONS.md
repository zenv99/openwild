# Photo permissions

Nobody has been contacted. Unlicensed photographs are not in the app. This note is only a list of who holds a real picture, and of the non-commercial photos already shipped that we would rather replace with CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA.

The two species below have no openly licensed photo of their own. The pack shows a labeled example of a close deadly relative. That example is not used as a fingerprint.

## Lepiota locanensis

Shipped picture: an example of **Lepiota brunneoincarnata**, photographed by Murselin Guney (Beyrek) at Mushroom Observer, CC BY-SA, [Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2015-10-11_Lepiota_brunneoincarnata_Chodat_%26_C._Mart%C3%ADn_564314.jpg). The caption in the app is "Example: Lepiota brunneoincarnata".

No photograph of *Lepiota locanensis* itself was found on Wikimedia Commons, iNaturalist (taxon 1564874, zero observations), GBIF media (taxon 213765458), or Mushroom Observer. There is therefore no image URL and no photographer to list.

The GBIF record gives the author as Espinosa, the place remark "Maule", and the bibliographic citation "Garrido 1985" (dataset taxon id macrofungi.CL.2023.10.0896). That citation is a name record, not a photograph, and no public contact email is attached to it.
